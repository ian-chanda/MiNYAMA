## Core Concepts Quick Reference

-   **Active Inference:** 
	  The principle that a system acts to minimize the discrepancy between its predictions and its sensory input. The objective is to `minimize(surprise)`.
-   **Data as a Graph:** 
	  We model the system as a network of nodes and edges to capture interconnectedness, as per Minyama's **Network View** principle.
-   **Human as a Sensor:** 
	  The state of human operators is a critical input, not an externality, as per the **Human-in-the-Loop** principle.
-   **Surprise as a Delta:** 
	  "Surprise" includes both unexpected events and the absence of expected ones, as per the **Baseline Establishment** principle.
