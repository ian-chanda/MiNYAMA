# Phase 5: The Analytical Output (Reports)

This directory holds the **Hardened Narrative**. It is the final destination for all modeling and observation data.

## 🔵 Analytical Tiers

### 1. [Analytical Outputs](analytical_outputs/)
Comprehensive reports on sector performance, cross-cutting insights, and strategic recommendations.

### 2. [Model Updates](model_updates/)
Specific documentation on how the world model was adjusted following a "Surprise" event.

### 3. [Surprise Analysis](surprise_analysis/)
Granular deep-dives into specific prediction errors and what they reveal about systemic vulnerabilities.

## 🛠 Hardening Standards
Reports move through three states of maturity:
*   **Level 1 (Skeletal)**: Headers mapped to Schema requirements.
*   **Level 2 (Functional)**: Data and surprising findings slotted in.
*   **Level 3 (Semantic)**: Professional justification and model-update recommendations.
