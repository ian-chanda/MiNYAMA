# MiNYAMA: Socio-Economic Modeling Interactive Dashboard

Welcome to the **MiNYAMA** (System for Socio-Economic Modeling and Analysis) repository. This system is designed as a "Source-Sync Structural Hardening" framework, where theoretical schemas are hardened into analytical insights through active inference.

---

## ✦ Navigation Hub: The Thinking Process

Explore the repository in the following sequence to align your mental model with the system's logic. This intentional structure is designed to guide you from foundational concepts to verified outputs.

### 🟢 [Phase 1: The Blueprint](schemas/)
**Start here.** This directory contains the foundational vocabulary and conceptual framework. Before looking at code, review these documents to understand the "rules of the world."
*   **What you'll find:** Core concepts, definitions, and relationships for each sector.
*   **Analogy:** The project's encyclopedia and rulebook.
*   **Sector Hubs:**
    *   **Agriculture**: [Blueprint](schemas/agriculture/Agriculture_Sector_Schema.md) | [Extensions](schemas/agriculture/README.md)
    *   **Education**: [Blueprint](schemas/education/Education_Sector_Schema.md) | [Extensions](schemas/education/README.md)
    *   **Finance**: [Blueprint](schemas/finance/Finance_Sector_Schema.md) | [Extensions](schemas/finance/README.md)
    *   **Healthcare**: [Blueprint](schemas/healthcare/Healthcare_Sector_Schema.md) | [Extensions](schemas/healthcare/README.md)
    *   **Manufacturing**: [Blueprint](schemas/manufacturing/Manufacturing_Sector_Schema.md) | [Extensions](schemas/manufacturing/README.md)
    *   **Retail**: [Blueprint](schemas/retail/Retail_Sector_Schema.md) | [Extensions](schemas/retail/README.md)

### 🟡 [Phase 2: The Engine](models/)
**Second stop.** See how the blueprint concepts are implemented as a software model. This is where the "thinking" happens.
*   **What you'll find:** Python logic for data representation (beliefs) and belief updating (inference).
*   **Analogy:** The mechanical brain that processes the encyclopedia to form conclusions.

### 🟠 [Phase 3: The Material Layer](evidence/)
**The Reality Check.** This is the objective data used to test the engine's predictions.
*   **What you'll find:** Raw data (Environmental Priors) and the observed "surprises" (Prediction Error Logs).
*   **Analogy:** The sensory input from the real world.

### 🔴 [Phase 4: The Action Layer](interventions/)
**Closing the Loop.** Documenting active changes and projects performed by the system or its agents.
*   **What you'll find:** Documented interventions and applied projects.
*   **Analogy:** The physical actions taken to influence the world model.

### 🔵 [Phase 5: The Analytical Output](reports/)
**The Library.** Review the final, "hardened" results of the modeling process.
*   **What you've built:** Insights generated by the delta between expectations and reality.
*   **Analogy:** Research papers and published works produced by the engine.

### 🔘 [Phase 6: The Integrity Layer](contracts/) & [Governance](governance/)
**The Audit.** Ensuring structural alignment and procedural verification.
*   **What you'll find:** Rules for syncing schemas to models and audit logs of system performance.
