# Repository Orientation Dashboard

Welcome. This repository is organized to be explored. Its structure is not arbitrary; it is a physical map of the system's thinking process, designed to guide you from foundational concepts to final outputs.

The goal is to make your exploration intuitive and predictable. By understanding the layout, you understand the system's logic.

---

## Your Path Through the Repository

This repository is arranged in a mandatory sequence. You are strongly encouraged to explore it in the following order.

### 1. `1_System_Schemas` - The Encyclopedia

**This is your starting point.**

This directory contains the project's foundational vocabulary and conceptual framework. Before you look at any code, review the documents here to understand the "rules of the world" as the system sees them.

*   **What you'll find:** The core concepts, definitions, and relationships that govern the entire system.
*   **Analogy:** Think of this as the project's encyclopedia and dictionary.

### 2. `2_Active_Inference_Model` - The Engine

**This is your second stop.**

Once you understand the core concepts, this directory shows you how they are implemented as a software model. This is the heart of the system where "thinking" happens.

*   **What you'll find:** The core Python source code, which is further divided into:
    *   `src/data_model/`: Code that represents the system's **beliefs** about the world.
    *   `src/inference_engine/`: Code that defines how the system **updates** those beliefs.
*   **Analogy:** If `1_System_Schemas` is the encyclopedia, this is the engine that reads it to form conclusions.

### 3. `3_Sector_Reports` - The Library

**This is your final destination.**

After observing the system's inputs and its engine, this directory shows you the results.

*   **What you'll find:** The outputs generated by the model, such as analyses, data visualizations, and formal reports.
*   **Analogy:** These are the research papers and published works produced by the engine.

---

## How to Engage with the System

The structure of the repository is your guide to contributing and making changes.

*   To understand or question a **foundational concept**, begin in `1_System_Schemas`.
*   To analyze or modify the **system's implementation**, explore `2_Active_Inference_Model`.
*   To review the **system's findings**, browse `3_Sector_Reports`.

This intentional structure is designed to make the consequences of any action legible and predictable. By following it, you are aligning your own mental model with the logic of the system itself.
